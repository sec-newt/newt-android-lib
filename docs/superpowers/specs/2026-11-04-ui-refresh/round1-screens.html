The 6 rules behind every mockup

1 · No text under 16

Kill the "8% smaller than Material" rule. Secondary lines (times, "Missed", sender) go 11→16sp. Names 21sp.

2 · One hero per screen

The biggest thing is the thing you came for: the caller, the clock, the newest message. Everything else steps down.

3 · Surfaces, not just outlines

Thin 1dp outlines on black vanish at low vision. Use a lifted surface (#0E1118) + 2–2.5dp borders so groups read as blocks.

4 · Never color alone

Spam/missed get a thick red edge stripe + icon + word. Senders get their color as a stripe and their name.

5 · Fill the dead space

Incoming call, Maps, Reader leave half the screen empty. Spend it on bigger targets and useful context.

6 · Thumb zone = action zone

Primary actions live in the bottom third, 60–84dp tall, with words. Status goes up top.

A · Phone → Recents

Status card shrinks to one line (it's a setting, not news). Names jump to 21sp, details to 16sp. Spam row gets a red edge stripe so it's spotted without reading. Call buttons become green rounded squares — bigger and clearly "call", not decoration.
